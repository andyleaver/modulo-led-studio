Historical basis used by the Era system:

- 1962: Nick Holonyak Jr. practical visible red LED.
- 1972: M. George Craford first yellow LED and much brighter red LED.
- 1993: efficient practical blue LED breakthrough.
- 1996: Nichia phosphor-converted white LED.
- 2010s: consumer LED app / effect-picker model as plateau before full system control.
